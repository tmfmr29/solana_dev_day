3 · Permissioning criteria

A wallet may hold or receive tNVDA only if the venue's compliance program records, on-chain, that its owner:

  • is a U.S. person;
  • has cleared identity verification (KYC) with the venue's provider;
  • is not subject to OFAC sanctions screening flags.

These checks are enforced by the Token-2022 transfer hook on every transfer of tNVDA, in any wallet or application, not only on this venue's interface. Criteria are applied by the venue's compliance officer through the admin console; every change is recorded in the audit trail with its transaction signature.

5 · Prohibited activity

  • No margin, credit or leverage. The venue does not extend credit or permit borrowing against positions.
  • No hypothecation. Venue assets are not lent, pledged or rehypothecated. Because tNVDA can only be held by whitelisted wallets, it cannot be deposited into lending protocols or other program-owned vaults that are not themselves permissioned; this restriction is enforced by the token's transfer hook, not by policy alone.
  • No short sales, no derivative instruments referencing tNVDA, and no spot transaction that is not fully funded by the investor's wallet at execution.

6 · Trading halts

  • tNVDA trading halts concurrently with any trading pause, Limit-Up/Limit-Down (LULD) interruption, market-wide circuit breaker, or regulatory suspension of NVDA initiated on its primary listing exchange (Nasdaq) or by FINRA, and resumes only when primary-market trading formally resumes.
  • The venue may also halt trading at the direction of a regulator or on its own compliance judgment.
  • Halts are executed on-chain and freeze every transfer of tNVDA, in every wallet and application, until lifted, so no off-venue or arbitrage execution is possible during a primary-market halt. Halt and resume events are recorded in the audit trail with their source and shown on the dashboard.
  • Prototype status: the primary-market feed is simulated on this devnet deployment; the on-chain halt mechanism it drives is fully implemented.

7 · Volume limits

tNVDA is treated as a Tier 1 tokenized NMS stock (NVDA is an S&P 500 constituent). Aggregate monthly venue volume in tNVDA is limited to —% of NVDA's consolidated average daily volume across all national securities exchanges over the preceding calendar month.

  • Month-to-date usage against the cap is published on the dashboard and monitored continuously from the trade tape by the venue's compliance monitor, which halts trading on-chain when the cap is reached.
  • A second breach within a rolling twelve-month period triggers a mandatory three-month pause in tNVDA trading.
  • Any breach is followed by an update to this notice within five business days, describing the breach and the actions taken.

8 · Transparency and reporting

  • Every executed trade is published to the public trade tape within 10 minutes of execution (masked wallet, size, USD price, reporting latency, transaction signature).
  • Pool address, liquidity, and daily volume are published in machine-readable form at /api/venue; the tape at /api/tape and /api/tape.csv. Data remain freely accessible for at least 30 days.

9 · MEV and market-integrity policy

  • Trades are constructed server-side at a fixed reference price and signed by the investor's wallet; there is no slippage parameter for a third party to exploit, so sandwich attacks cannot change execution price.
  • Production submission goes through a private RPC / fair-ordering endpoint to prevent front-running by validators or searchers.
  • The venue monitors the tape for wash trading and manipulative patterns; suspected activity is reported and the wallets involved are suspended pending review.

10 · Books and records

The venue retains complete trading records and compliance documentation (trade tape, whitelisting changes, halt events, venue settings) for the duration of its exemption and at least three years thereafter, in human-readable and machine-readable form, available to SEC staff on request. See audit trail (CSV).

11 · Investor identity and AML

Fiat on-ramp is provided by MoonPay, which performs identity verification and sanctions screening before delivering USDC to the investor's wallet. The venue binds the verified identity to the wallet it whitelists. Transfers at or above $3,000 are subject to Travel Rule information sharing with counterparties' VASPs (not implemented in this prototype).
